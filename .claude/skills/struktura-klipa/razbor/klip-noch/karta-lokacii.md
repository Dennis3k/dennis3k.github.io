# Карта локации

## Чем отличается от карты персонажа

Принципиально одним. **Карту персонажа мы светили нейтрально** — ровный дневной свет, серый фон: её задача зафиксировать личность, а контровой закат личность как раз прячет.

**Карту локации, наоборот, надо светить ровно тем закатом, что в клипе.** Здесь свет — это и есть опознавательный признак места. Локация без своего света — просто меловые скалы, каких в Англии много.

И второе, что карта обязана зафиксировать: **геометрию**. Где гряда относительно моря, где солнце относительно гряды, с какой стороны обрыв. Если это не закрепить, каждый кадр изобретёт собственное побережье, и клип рассыплется на десять разных мест.

## Промт

```
Create a single location reference sheet for a film production. One place, four views, one horizontal image divided into four panels of equal size separated by thin neutral gaps. No people in any panel.

THE PLACE — the chalk cliffs at Seven Sisters, East Sussex, England, taken from the attached reference images. Keep the geography identical in every panel and identical to the references:
a chain of white chalk headlands running away from the viewer in a long curve toward the horizon, each rounded crest lower and hazier than the last;
sheer vertical chalk faces, bright white, streaked with horizontal grey flint bands, weathered and rain-channelled;
a narrow shingle beach at their feet with a thin white line of surf;
a clifftop carpeted in low coastal scrub, dark green wind-flattened shrubs and dry bleached grass, with bare chalk and rock showing through at the very edge;
a broad calm sea filling the open side.

THE LIGHT — identical in all four panels and identical to the references. Golden hour, late evening. The sun is a low orange disc resting just above the sea horizon, on the LEFT, and it stays in exactly that position in every panel where it is visible. A long broken column of molten gold runs from it across the water toward the viewer. The sky is stratified: deep orange at the horizon rising through amber into rose and dusty violet at the top, with scattered clouds lit along their undersides in orange and pink. The chalk faces take warm orange light on the sunward side and fall into cool blue-violet shadow on the other. The scrub and grass glow warm amber. Air is clear with light atmospheric haze toward the horizon.

THE FOUR PANELS:
PANEL 1 — wide establishing view from an elevated position along the coast, showing the whole chain of headlands curving away, the bay, the beach, the sea and the sun together, so that the geography of the place is unmistakable.
PANEL 2 — the clifftop itself at standing height, the camera low among the scrub, showing the ground he will sit on: shrubs, dry grass, exposed chalk, the lip of the drop, and the sea beyond it.
PANEL 3 — the open sea and the sun, long lens, the horizon a clean hard line, the sun disc low and large, the glitter column compressed down the centre of the frame. No land.
PANEL 4 — the foot of a cliff at water level: the sheer white chalk wall rising out of frame, wet and dark at its base, a swell lifting and breaking into white foam across wet shingle and a flat chalk shelf, fine spray hanging backlit in the air.

TECHNICAL — photorealistic landscape photography. Not illustration, not 3D render, not painting. Natural colour, deep focus, fine natural grain, highlights that roll off rather than clipping, shadows that keep detail.

NEGATIVE: people, figures, silhouettes of people, boats, ships, buildings, houses, lighthouses, roads, fences, footpaths, signs, any text, labels, captions, panel titles, watermark, logo, signature, different sun positions between panels, sun on the right, two suns, midday light, blue hour, night, overcast sky, storm, tropical turquoise water, sandy beach, palm trees, illustration, 3D render, cartoon, HDR halos, over-sharpening, oversaturation, clipped highlights, crushed blacks.
```
